Three steps.
One test dig.

The size of the ball is known, so every potato in the photo is measured against it. That is all the calibration you need.

  1. 01

    Place the ball

    Dig up the plants, spread the potatoes in a single layer and put the blue ball in the middle.

  2. 02

    Take one photo

    Open the app, hold your phone in landscape and keep the potatoes inside the frame.

  3. 03

    Read the report

    Within minutes you have a PDF with grading, count, yield and forecasts.

The report

A PDF you can act on.
