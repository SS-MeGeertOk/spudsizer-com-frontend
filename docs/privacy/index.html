Last updated: 10 October 2026

This privacy statement explains which personal data Spudsizer processes when you visit our website www.spudsizer.com or contact us, why we do so, and what rights you have.

We do not have a data protection officer. For questions about privacy, email info@spudsizer.nl.

2. What data we process

Contact form

When you fill in the form on our website, we receive:

  • your name
  • your company name (if you fill it in)
  • your phone number (if you fill it in)
  • your email address
  • your message (if you fill it in)
  • the language of the page on which you filled in the form

Email and phone

When you email or call us, we process the information you give us.

Visiting the website

Each visit involves technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the visit. This is needed to show you the website and keep it secure. We do not use analytics tools and do not track which pages you view.

Spam protection

The form is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile. Turnstile uses technical data about your browser and device, and your IP address, to check that a person is filling in the form.

3. Why we use this data

  • To contact you about your question or order, and to prepare an order. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in answering your question.
  • To protect the form against spam and abuse. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure website.
  • To deliver and secure the website. Legal basis: our legitimate interest.

We do not use your data for newsletters or advertising, and we never sell it to anyone.

4. Who receives your data

We use a few service providers that process data on our behalf. They may only use your data for the service they provide to us.

  • GitHub (GitHub Pages): website hosting.
  • Cloudflare: spam protection (Turnstile) and forwarding the form.
  • Resend: delivering the form to our mailbox as an email.
  • Our email provider: storing email in our mailbox.

Otherwise we only share your data when the law requires us to.

5. Data outside the European Union

GitHub, Cloudflare and Resend are US companies, so your data may also be processed outside the European Economic Area. This is done on the basis of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses, so that your data is adequately protected.

6. How long we keep your data

  • Data from the contact form and your emails: up to 2 years after our last contact, unless you become a customer.
  • If you become a customer: as long as needed for the contract. Records in our administration, such as invoices, are kept for 7 years because Dutch tax law requires it.
  • Technical data for hosting and spam protection: only as long as the provider needs it for security.

7. Cookies

Our website does not use tracking or advertising cookies, or any analytics tools. That is why we do not ask you to consent to cookies.

When you choose a language on the website, your browser remembers that choice in its local storage. This is only used to show the website in your language and is never sent to us.

According to Cloudflare, Turnstile does not use cookies to collect or store information.

8. Security

The website uses a secure connection (https). Only the people at Spudsizer who need your data to help you have access to it.

9. Automated decisions

We do not make decisions about you that are taken solely by a computer, and we do not create profiles of you.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data. You can also object to the use of your data, ask us to restrict its use, or request your data in a portable file.

Send your request to info@spudsizer.nl. We reply within one month. To make sure the request comes from you, we may ask you to confirm who you are.

Do you have a complaint about how we handle your data? Please contact us first. You also have the right to file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) via autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl.

11. Changes

We may update this privacy statement, for example when our website or services change. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
